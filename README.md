### Markets

**[board-network-brazil](https://github.com/Appa019/board-network-brazil)** maps who sits
on the boards of Brazilian listed companies, and how much power those overlaps
concentrate. Neo4j for the graph, NetworKit for the metrics, Node2Vec for link
prediction, over public CVM filings from 2021 to 2025.

**[cvm-analyzer](https://github.com/Appa019/cvm-analyzer)** reads a Brazilian regulatory
filing and checks it against the 156 items of CVM Resolution 80. It also diffs two
versions of the same document by meaning rather than by line, and the chat always cites
the passage it answered from.

**[portfolio-ai](https://github.com/Appa019/portfolio-ai)** puts portfolio decisions
through three tiers of agents sitting on an ensemble of XGBoost, BiLSTM-Attention and a
Temporal Fusion Transformer. Live B3 data, walk-forward validation.

### Models

**[ai-image-detector](https://github.com/Appa019/ai-image-detector)** tells you whether an
image or a video was generated. Nine models vote, a meta-learner decides, and what comes
back is a confidence score with the forensic evidence behind it.

**[slm-setup-wizard](https://github.com/Appa019/slm-setup-wizard)** walks you through
fine-tuning a small language model on a single GPU, or on Colab if you do not have one.
LoRA and 4-bit quantization.

### Built to explain something

**[rag-and-mcp-explained](https://github.com/Appa019/rag-and-mcp-explained)** is a visual
essay on what actually happens inside RAG, and on where MCP stops being just another API
call.

**[llm-visualizer](https://github.com/Appa019/llm-visualizer)** walks through the
transformer, one interactive step at a time.

### Side projects

**[petshop-scheduler](https://github.com/Appa019/petshop-scheduler)** is 8Patas, a
veterinary platform that identifies the breed from a photo and builds the preventive care
schedule from there.

**[smart-plant-controller](https://github.com/Appa019/smart-plant-controller)** keeps a
